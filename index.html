Whatever it says above your door

Five businesses. Five different days.

Every business runs differently, so there's no single package here. This is how the same three tools bend around five very different working days.

New to this? What a website, an AI assistant and automation actually do Read moreShow less
A website
Your shop window online. When someone hears about you or searches for what you do, it's where they find what you offer, what it costs, where you are and how to book or get in touch, at any time of day.
An AI assistant
A chat box on your website that answers customers' questions in plain, friendly language, using only the information you've given it: your services, prices, hours and policies. If it doesn't know something, it says so and passes the question to you.
Automation
Small jobs that happen by themselves once they're set up, like a reminder the day before an appointment or a thank-you message afterwards. You decide what gets sent, and when.

The things people usually ask first

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. You don't touch any of the technical side. I handle the building, hosting and updates. You just tell me about your work.

Who hosts the website?

I do, as part of the care plan. It stays online, secure and up to date without you having to think about it.

Can I keep my own domain and booking tool?

Yes. If you already have a domain, we use it. And if you've got a booking system you like, such as Square, Fresha or Calendly, the site wires straight into it. You keep your tools, and your bookings keep landing where they always have.

What if I don't have a booking system?

That's completely fine, and more common than you'd think. The site sends enquiries straight to wherever you actually read them: your inbox, WhatsApp or your phone. If you decide you want proper online booking later, we can add it then.

How long does it take?

Usually a week or two from our first chat to going live, sometimes faster. You'll see a working demo early on, so there are no surprises.
