Questions

The things practitioners usually ask first.

Do I need to be technical?

Not at all. You don't touch any of the technical side — I handle the building, hosting and updates. You just tell me about your work.

Who hosts the website?

I do, as part of the care plan. It stays online, secure and up to date without you having to think about it.

Can I keep my own domain and booking tool?

Yes. If you already have a domain, we use it. The site wires into your existing Square booking — you keep your tools and your bookings.

What does the assistant cost to run?

It's £99 to set up and £25/mo, which covers running it on your site. There's nothing else to pay or manage on your end.

How long does it take?

Usually a week or two from our first chat to going live — sometimes faster. You'll see a working demo early on, so there are no surprises.
