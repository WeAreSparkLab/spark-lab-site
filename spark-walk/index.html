How it works

Simpler than you think.
Easy to make your own.

01

Open the app

Create a free account, then open Spark Walk in your browser. You can add it to your home screen for easy access.

02

Press start and walk

A sound cue tells you when to speed up and when to ease off. No counting, no watching the screen, no thinking. Just walk and follow the rhythm.

03

When your walk ends

You're done. Notice how the pace felt, then choose another walk when it suits you.

Features

Built for real life.
Not Instagram life.

🎵

Sound-guided intervals

A gentle audio cue tells you when to change pace. Phone stays in your pocket. Eyes stay on the world around you. No counting required.

👟

Default or custom walks

Follow the default 21-minute interval walk or build your own — adjust interval lengths, total duration, and pace levels to suit you.

👥

Group walks with anyone

Invite friends or other users to walk together — even if you're miles apart. Stay in sync, build accountability, and make moving social.

🌍

Works anywhere in the world

Spark Walk uses your device's location to work wherever you are — park, street, beach, or treadmill. No region restrictions.

📱

Add to your home screen

Works like a native app. Add to your phone's home screen for instant one-tap access — no app store required, no storage used.

🆓

Completely free

No subscription, no premium tier, no catch. Spark Walk is free for everyone because good health shouldn't cost money to access.
