Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 Aug 2025

We respect your privacy. Spark Quotes stores only what’s needed to run the app: your favorites, settings, and (optionally) an account email if you sign in. We don’t sell or share personal data.

  • Analytics: basic, privacy-friendly analytics to understand usage and improve the product.
  • Notifications: opt-in. You can disable anytime in settings or your device preferences.
  • Data deletion: you can delete your account and related data at any time from the app’s Settings, or email us.
