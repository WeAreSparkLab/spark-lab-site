Introducing SparkLab Studio: AI-Powered Websites & Automation for Small Businesses

If you run a small business, your website should be doing more than sitting there. SparkLab Studio builds websites that come with automation built in — so booking, follow-ups, reviews, and FAQs run themselves instead of running you.

The problem

Most small business websites are static. They look fine, but every actual task — chasing a no-show, answering routine customer questions, matching someone to the right service, asking for a review — still lands back on the owner.

What's included

SparkLab Studio combines a custom website build with a set of AI assistants you add as needed:

  • Booking assistant
  • Service matcher
  • No-show saver
  • Review collector
  • Review responder
  • FAQ answerer
  • Intake summariser
  • Content helper

Each assistant is priced and added individually, so you're not paying for automation you don't need yet.

Case study: Roots Wellbeing

Ruth, owner of Roots Wellbeing, was the first business built on this model. A calm one-page site with Square booking built in, hosting and go-live handled end to end. It went from first call to live site in one week. You can see her live site linked from this page as case study #1.

Who it's for

Small, solo-run service businesses today, with multi-location businesses next as the studio grows. If you're a trade business (plumbing, electrical, etc.), this isn't built for you — the workflows here are specific to appointment-based service work.

Pricing

Website builds are a fixed cost, agreed up front, from £450, plus a £20/month care plan. AI assistants come in three monthly plans, from £97/month, because they keep saving you time every week they run — not just on day one.
