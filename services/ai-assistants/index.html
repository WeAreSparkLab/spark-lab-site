Practical help for busy days

An extra helping hand for the questions and admin that keep coming back.

SparkLab's AI assistants use the information you supply about your business. They can help customers find answers and help you prepare replies, while you stay in control of what they know.

Try the live demos

Choose the jobs that would actually help.

There is no need to add everything at once. Start with a website, then choose assistants that fit the way your customers contact you and the work you want to make easier.

Answer familiar questions

A booking assistant or FAQ answerer can use your services, prices, hours and policies to help visitors, including when you are busy.

Guide an enquiry

A service matcher can ask a few questions and point someone towards a suitable service from the options you provide.

Help with follow-ups

Tools can draft reminders, review responses or content in your voice for you to check before use.

Your information comes first.

The assistant is set up around your business details. If it lacks an answer, it should say so and pass the question to you. The examples on the SparkLab homepage use fictional businesses so you can see how these tools work without sharing a real customer's information.

Questions people ask

Will an assistant make up my prices or policies?

It is configured to answer from the information you provide. When something is missing, it should say so or hand the question back to you.

Do I have to use every assistant?

No. You can choose the tools that suit your business, and add more later if you want them.

Can I try one before deciding?

Yes. The SparkLab homepage has live examples you can use to understand what each assistant does.
